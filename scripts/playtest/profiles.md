# Playtest player profiles (EASY TO CUSTOMISE: edit, add or remove profiles here)

1. **achiever** — "The Achiever". Wants every achievement and colour style. Checks Records often. Gets annoyed when one takes forever or the requirement is unclear. Uses /autoplay to grind, but notices how long things take.
2. **fighter** — "The Fighter". Loves combat mastery: wants to beat hard bots, learn every kaiju's KAIJU power, find strong strategies. Judges whether fights feel fair, readable and skill-based, and whether there's depth.
3. **story** — "The Story Fan". Plays the Story campaigns (The Grand Feast, then Second Helpings): dialogue, cutscenes, chapter difficulty ramp, bosses, rewards. Judges whether the story is charming and the difficulty curve fair.
4. **newbie** — "The First-Timer". Has never played. Starts from the title screen, reads How to Play, tries Free Play. Judges how clear the menus, controls and goals are, and how fast it becomes fun.
5. **collector** — "The Collector". Wants to unlock every kaiju, table and colour, and fill the Gallery. Judges whether unlocks feel rewarding and how long the collection takes.
6. **host** — "The Party Host". Imagines running game night for 4-6 friends. Explores Party Modes (Team Feast, Crumb Rush, Hot Dumpling...), Party Cup, the setup screen, the Online lobby. Judges how easy it is to set up a group game and how fun each mode is.
7. **tinkerer** — "The Tinkerer". Explores Settings, twists (mutators), power-ups, hazards, the Table Workshop, keyboard controls. Judges how much variety and customisation there is and whether it works.
8. **challenger** — "The Challenge Seeker". Goes straight for the hardest content: Gauntlet, Daily Challenge, Spicy chapters, Dessert Course, hard bots. Judges whether challenges are hard but fair and give a reason to come back.
9. **kid** — "The 9-Year-Old". Short attention span, loves silly characters, gets frustrated by losing too often or by reading lots of text. Judges fun, humour, readability and frustration.
10. **qa** — "The QA Tester". Hunts bugs: odd button sequences, Escape mid-match, switching screens fast, text overflow, things that don't respond, JavaScript errors (/errors). Judges polish and stability.

# Playtest evaluation — challenger ("The Challenge Seeker")

**Fun score: 6 / 10** (Fun, readable, snappy rounds, but the hardest content is either too swingy (Hot Dumpling daily) or too short/shallow (Gauntlet, Spicy Ch1), and I never got to use the KAIJU mechanic in a real fight.)

## What I did
- Gauntlet x3 attempts (played by hand): reached wave 3, 3, then died on 4 ("Best wave 3"). 
- Daily Challenge (today = HOT DUMPLING, 4 players): 3 tries (1 via auto:hard on my seat, 2 by hand). Best "2 rounds", never won. Learned to pass the dumpling with shove.
- Story: Ch1 cleared by hand with 3 stars, then Spicy Ch1 replay cleared by hand (trivial). Ch2-6 attempted via /autoplay hard (harness bot = "time passing"; NOT my own play): Ch2 clear, Ch3 defeat, Ch4 (boss) clear, Ch5 clear, Ch6 defeat, Ch7 aborted by harness, Ch8 defeat. Final: Story 22%, 10/36 stars, 1/12 spicy.
- Did not reach Dessert Course or Spicy beyond Ch1 (progression-gated). ~9 screenshots viewed. /errors: none.

## What matched my goals / what was fun
- Short rounds (~10-20 s) make every loss feel like "one more try".
- Hot Dumpling is a great daily format: shoving to pass the bomb creates real tension and a skill answer. I improved from 0 rounds to 2 rounds by learning it.
- Gauntlet pace ramps (1 rival, 2, 3...), tips on the countdown screen are helpful.
- Spicy toggle ("tougher rivals") is clear and gives a "Feeling Spicy!" trophy; the Daily results board (rank, rounds, KOs) is nice.
- Cutscenes/art are charming; art and HUD are readable.

## What took too long / was tedious
- Story intro/chapter cutscenes need ~5 Space presses every launch, including on replays of the same chapter.
- Gauntlet waves 1-3 are near-free (rival died within one 3s action), so the first minutes are filler before it gets hard.

## What was over too quickly / too shallow
- Gauntlet is brief: I died around wave 4 with no upgrades/picks between waves, and there is no sense of what a "good" wave count is (no target, no reward preview). Little reason to return.
- Spicy Ch1 was beaten in about 10 s of play with the same "attack" command; "tougher rivals" did not feel tougher at the start of the game.
- Round length means the KAIJU loop (crumbs -> 100 belly -> STOMP -> power) almost never happens: belly peaked at ~81 and typically 9-45 before the round ended. In the hardest modes the signature mechanic is effectively irrelevant; hard bots just win with cutlery/swipes.
- Daily: only a single mode of the day and one outcome (win/fail). Three tries burned in minutes with no daily reward visible.

## Confusing or unclear
- Daily FAILED screen: my seat is "BUBBLEGUMBO" in Hot Dumpling but "GOBZILLA" in Gauntlet/Story. In the results list I looked at GOBZILLA (a bot) ranked #1, and it was unclear which row was me, and why "FAILED". Please highlight "YOU" in the standings.
- It is not stated on the Daily screen what the win condition is (first to how many rounds? "best 2 rounds" of what target?).
- Hot Dumpling: when I hold it there is no obvious on-screen hint of which button passes it (shove) other than the banner "HOT! PASS IT!".
- Chapter locks: map shows "Beat the chapter before to unlock" only after selecting; the Ch3 DEFEATED and Ch4 playing sequence in my run looked like I could play Ch4 without clearing Ch3 (possibly a stale-screen harness issue; unsure).
- Ch2 3 rounds "Spicy: clear it first" - fine, but Spicy is gated to first clear so a challenge seeker cannot start with it.

## Bugs found
- /errors: none.
- Possible: game screen stayed on "game" with "DEFEATED" banner after autoplay and the Story map showed different state than the banner; probably harness. No reproducible bug confirmed.

## Top 3 changes that would raise my score
1. Give Gauntlet and hard content real stakes: between-wave pick-one upgrades, a boss wave every 5, ghost/target waves, and a streak/daily-leaderboard-style reward so each deeper wave matters.
2. Make Spicy and Hard actually harder from the start (spicy rivals using powers/KAIJU, faster aggression, modifiers) and let the player toggle Spicy without a first clear if they are experienced.
3. Make KAIJU matter in tough content: faster crumbs/belly or round timers long enough that stomping happens in 1-in-2 rounds; also clearly mark "YOU" on result boards and explain the Daily win condition and goal up front.

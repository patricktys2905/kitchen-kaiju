# Playtest evaluation — The 9-Year-Old

**Fun score: 7 / 10** (Gorgeous, silly characters and a satisfying "pop" of KO-ing things, but the bots were pushovers, the video cutscenes plus tap-through dialogue made me wait, and nothing told me what the buttons did.)

## What I did
- Title -> Free Play: joined, cycled kaiju, ended up on Toastbat (toast bat!), added 2 bots (accidentally set difficulty to EASY by pressing "2"), played a full match (7 rounds, won 5-1-1).
- Used attack/shove/dash/crumbs/stomp/power in a mixed, mashy way. Went KAIJU once (and then got KO'd by "cheese" - the fondue table hazard - the same round).
- Opened the KAIJU Gallery: 16 characters, 4 locked "???", costume colours.
- Story: skipped the opening video, read Crabbage's dialogue, beat Chapter 1 (2 rounds), saw Chapter Clear screen, then beat Chapter 2 (4 players). About 25 minutes of play-time equivalent.
- /errors: none.

## What matched my goals / what was fun
- The characters are GREAT. Toastbat, Gobzilla the strawberry lizard, Mothball, Crabbage with a cabbage shell, hotdog hedgehog Hotchog, bubblegum elephant. I would pick one just because it's funny.
- Tables look like real toys: ice cream parlour with giant scoops, fondue night, breakfast rush with toasters. Each round a new one - keeps it fresh.
- Big loud text ("FIRST BITE!", "MOTHBALL WINS THE ROUND", "TOASTBAT got TOASTED") and funny KO captions ("skewered") - readable and funny.
- "Instant replay" after a round and the SHARE button are cool.
- Dying to a toaster ("got TOASTED") is hilarious even when I lose.
- Rounds are short (a few seconds to ~10s), so I never sat around.
- Story chapter 1 is easy and teaches throwing; Crabbage's trash-talk is in a big speech bubble with a big picture, which I'd actually read.

## What took too long / was tedious
- Opening cutscene + chapter intro video + dialogue before the very first fight: 4-5 taps before I play. A kid would mash SKIP and miss the point.
- Round-end -> "Chapter clear" -> unlock screen -> next chapter intro cutscene -> dialogue is a lot of waiting between fights.
- After a match there's a pause with an Instant Replay before you can do anything.

## What was over too quickly / too shallow
- On Easy/Normal bots I won almost every round in about 3 seconds with one skewer; it felt like I was not really being challenged, so the fun came from the look, not the fight. Chapter 1 and 2 were both over in a couple of rounds.
- Going KAIJU (the big transformation) happened once and I barely noticed anything - it should feel HUGE and be the best part.

## Confusing or unclear
- Free Play setup screen is a wall of keyboard hints (WASD, SPACE, F, L-SHIFT...) and tiny text. "THROW to join" is not obvious; clicking the empty slot did nothing visible for me - I had to press a key. A kid on a touchscreen wouldn't know.
- Key "2" quietly changed bot difficulty to EASY ("2 bots" label looks like it adds bots). Easy to change by accident.
- Picking a kaiju: arrow keys did nothing on the setup screen; only A/D worked, which isn't explained until you've joined.
- I didn't know why I died to "cheese" - hazard warnings weren't obvious while mashing buttons.
- "Hold THROW to charge" is only mentioned in Chapter 1's speech bubble.

## Bugs found
- None in /errors.
- Minor: In Free Play, clicking "THROW to join" (112,384) did not add a player; only pressing Space did (could be harness-only, but a tap/click on that button should work for kids on tablets).
- Minor: after the Chapter 2 win the "CHAPTER CLEAR!" overlay stayed over the table with a SHARE button and a giant strawberry tail sliding in from the right - fine but a little cluttered.

## Top 3 changes that would raise my score
1. Make the fights harder to win by default on even the first few chapters (bots that actually grab and throw at me), and make going KAIJU feel like a huge, screen-shaking, "I'm a giant now!" payoff.
2. Cut the wait: skip/shorten cutscenes by default after the first time, auto-continue from Chapter Clear, and put a big "PLAY AGAIN" right on the result screen.
3. Make the Free Play setup kid-simple: tap-to-join that works, big arrows to pick a kaiju that respond to the arrow keys, fewer keyboard hints, and no accidental difficulty change from the number keys.

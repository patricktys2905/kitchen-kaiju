# Playtest evaluation — kid (The 9-Year-Old)

**Fun score: 7 / 10** (silly, gorgeous characters and quick punchy rounds; held back by text-heavy story intros, a very easy early game, and no clear feedback on why I win/lose.)

## What I did
- Title screen -> Free Play. Cycled the character picker (Gobzilla Jr., Squidgeon, Crabbage, picked Bloblin the jelly) and added 2 bots. Played one full match (to 5 rounds, ~9 rounds) and won 5-2-1 with mixed attack/shove/dash/stomp/power.
- Story: chapters 1, 2, 3 cleared (ch1 3 stars, ch2 2 stars, ch3 1 star), then lost the ch4 boss (Norigon) 0-2 on the first try.
- Opened the Kaiju Gallery: 16/20 unlocked, clicked silly ones (Dumplord, Chocomunch), saw locked "???" with unlock hints.
- ~65 calls. /errors: none.

## What matched my goals / what was fun
- The characters are the star: a strawberry dinosaur, a jelly with a cherry, a dumpling lord, a hot-dog hedgehog, a sloth on a baguette. The "wins the round" splash with the big angry kaiju portrait is great. A kid would show this to a friend.
- Rounds are short (seconds to ~15s). Always something happening, so I never got bored mid-match.
- Round-end callouts ("DOUBLE SKEWER!", "STOLEN!", "REVENGE IS SWEET!", "BLOBLIN skewered AVOCADRAKE") are funny and readable.
- End-of-match awards ("Butterfingers", "Crumb Monster", "Forked up: score 25 knockouts") are fun and give bragging stuff.
- Boss (Norigon) was the first moment of real tension: he goes giant and squashes me. Losing to him felt like "ok, again!" not unfair.
- Gallery: each kaiju has its own power + trait, locked ones show how to unlock; the "NEXT UP" goal line on the title gives a clear target (Minturtle, Lime colour).
- Kaiju chatter in story ("Hehe. Wobble wobble.") is cute.

## What took too long / was tedious
- Story chapter intros: an opening cutscene with slowly typed text, then 2-4 dialogue boxes before EVERY chapter. A 9-year-old would mash Skip. Skip Chat (S) only seemed to skip one box at a time, so I pressed it repeatedly.
- "Chapter clear" -> NEXT -> dialogue -> Enter is a lot of clicks between matches.
- First to 5 rounds in Free Play is fairly long when I'm already winning easily.

## What was over too quickly / too shallow
- Chapters 1-3 and the Free Play bots were very easy: spamming "attack" won most rounds and I got 3 stars on ch1 without trying. Rounds sometimes ended in about 3 seconds (KO on the first swipe).
- Stomp/power barely mattered in the easy fights; I couldn't tell when they helped. Dash/shove vs attack felt similar.
- Free Play has 15 tables but I didn't notice big differences between them beyond looks (conveyor on the sushi table was the exception).

## Confusing or unclear
- Opening-chapter tip ("Hold THROW to charge, let go to fling") is read-heavy and I would never read it; no on-screen controls hint shown in match start beyond a tip line under the "1" countdown.
- Hard to tell what the belly bar / stars on the top-left cards mean without reading.
- The "SPICY: clear it first" toggle and the REWARDS button weren't explained.
- Free Play kaiju picker: arrows are small, easy to miss; bot seats say "click: add a bot" but it's not obvious you have to press PLAY (it is, at the bottom).
- Being knocked out is shown as just a greyed top card; I watched the rest of the round with "INSTANT REPLAY / FAST FORWARD" text, which is fine but a bit wordy.

## Bugs found
- None seen; /errors returned "none".
- Minor: during the story hub, pressing Enter right after the chapter-clear screen often just reopened a dialogue box rather than starting the match, so it took 2-3 key presses (could be harness timing).

## Top 3 changes that would raise my score
1. Make story intros skippable in one press (and shorter/optional), with a "Next chapter -> straight into match" button after a win.
2. Raise early difficulty/variety (smarter bots in ch1-3 and Free Play "Normal") and make stomp/power moments obviously cool so mashing attack isn't enough; add short big-text prompts like "BELLY FULL! PRESS STOMP!".
3. Let me pick my silly kaiju faster and show it off: bigger picker arrows, a "random kaiju" per round, and a little reaction animation/sound when I win or get squashed.

# Playtest evaluation: The Story Fan

**Fun score: 6.5 / 10** (charming cast, great chapter-clear and defeat cards and a clever "what beat you" coach, but the campaign is the same 2-4 bot brawl with a new face on it every time: no story structure, no boss fights that feel like bosses, and nothing in the story I would want to chase)

> Screenshots are in `C:\Users\Pat\kitchen-kaiju\art-src\.playtest\r6-7103\` (shot-N.png). Harness port 7103. About 75 commands.

## What I did
- Title -> Story (The Grand Feast). Watched the opening cutscene (a storybook shot of Gobzilla/Bloblin/Blueberry-bear style trio eyeing the Golden Spoon, shot-1), the SCENE/banter before each match (Squidgeon: "Four cups, two of us, one of you. Do the maths!"), and every chapter-clear card.
- Chapter 1 (Crabbage, easy): won 2-1 (2 stars). Chapter 2 (Squidgeon + Bloblin): 2-0, 3 stars. Chapter 3 (Leftovers, 3 rivals): won 2-1 with 1 star. Chapter 4 BOSS Norigon + Salmonster: lost 3 times (cutlery/KAIJU squash, then Giant Hand twice), took the mercy "Helping Hand" button and won. Unlocked Minturtle. Chapter 5 (Party Crashers, first to 3): lost once to a normal bot, won with a hard bot.
- Checked Story Rewards (shot-22), Pantry (shot-24), Records (shot-25), Gallery (shot-27), title screen "NEXT UP".
- 11 matches, about 6 wins. Never reached Second Helpings or Dessert Course (chapters 6-12 and the final-boss foreshadowing were not reached in the budget, so I cannot judge them). /errors was empty at start, middle and end.

## What worked for me (briefly)
- Chapter clear cards are the best thing in the campaign: rival quote that reacts to how I won ("Not even ONE round for me?!" after a 3-star, "That was close, though. Next time!" after a 1-star), plus a chef line, tokens and mastery (shot-5).
- "What beat you" on the defeat card is genuinely useful and specific (KAIJU squash vs Giant Hand vs thrown cutlery) and changed between my losses (shot-15, shot-17, shot-21).
- Pre-match banter (SKIP CHAT) gives each rival a voice in one line. The "Next course: X. Y is already sharpening the cutlery..." line is a charming chapter handoff.
- The NEW KAIJU reveal for Minturtle (shot-20) is a proper reward moment.
- The map layout with crown nodes at 4/8/12 and the red-bordered boss tiles reads well (shot-2).

## The single biggest structural problem
**The Story is a ladder of identical matches, not a campaign.** Every chapter is "win first-to-2 or 3 rounds against 1-3 bots on a different table". The only thing that changes is the rival portrait, the table and the bot level. Bosses are the clearest example: chapter 4 "Boss of the Belt" was just Norigon (a normal bot that starts with a full belly) plus Salmonster, with the same round loop, and I lost three times to the same two generic things (Giant Hand, KAIJU squash) that every non-story match also has. Nothing about it was a set-piece. I never felt a plot: the opening shows a Golden Spoon, then I got eleven rivals who each say a line and leave; no rival returns, nobody reacts to my kaiju, my choices change nothing, and Second Helpings/Dessert are not signposted from the map at all (I could not even find how to reach them from the Story screen: the MODES button simply returned me to the title).

It matters for my profile because a Story Fan plays for escalation, recurring characters, set-pieces and a payoff. Here the reward for finishing chapters is mostly a kaiju I mostly already have (Gallery shows 17/20 kaiju unlocked at only 18% story, so story-gated unlocks are 4 mystery kaiju and 3 tables) and a number of stars. The economy also does not back the story up (see Proposal 3): tokens are paid by match length, so my flawless 3-star ch 2 paid +44 while a long, scrappy loss paid +58 and a 5-round win paid +183.

## Big change proposals (most important first)

### Proposal 1: Boss Fights as Set-Piece "Courses" (scripted multi-phase bosses with their own arena rules)
- **Problem it solves:** Chapter 4 boss Norigon was a regular bot with a full starting belly. I lost to it three times without ever being taught anything about *it* (the defeat card told me about KAIJU squash and the Giant Hand in general, shot-15/17). The crown chapters 4, 8 and 12 are the structural spikes of the campaign and they are not distinct.
- **How it would work (detailed design):**
  - Every crown chapter (4, 8, 12 of the Grand Feast; 3 of the 6 Second Helpings chapters; Dessert Course finale) becomes a **Boss Course** with 3 phases instead of "first to 2 rounds". Total 3 rounds, each round is one phase; losing a round restarts only that phase (3 attempts of phase before the card offers Helping Hand).
  - Screen flow: map -> boss intro cutscene (existing SCENE system, 3 lines) -> **Phase card** (a 2-second title card: "PHASE 1 · THE CONVEYOR" with the boss name and a one-line telegraph tip) -> round -> **mid-boss quip** (boss line, 3 s, skippable) -> next phase -> clear card.
  - Chapter 4 example (Norigon, sushi bar): P1 "Warm-up Roll": Norigon is a normal bot but the conveyor runs 1.5x speed and drops sushi pieces that can be thrown. P2 "KAIJU Rice": Norigon is permanently KAIJU for the first 12 s with a visible red windup ring (existing stomp ring) and Salmonster is the one you must KO to end the phase. P3 "Wasabi Rush": Norigon at 60% of the table (sushi plate hazards shrink the arena by 25% every 10 s). Each phase has ONE stated way to win (KO target, survive timer, or push boss off a marked edge) shown top-centre with a progress bar.
  - Numbers: boss HP is not needed; each phase round is capped at 45 s (so the first 4/8/12 boss run stays under 3 minutes). Rewards: first clear gives 3 stars as normal; each phase cleared without losing a life = 1 "boss medal" shown on the node (3 medals instead of an unrelated star formula on boss chapters).
  - Boss-only tip line replaces generic defeat tip: "What beat you: Norigon's wasabi charge. Hit him from the side while his ring is red."
- **Example moment:** I enter chapter 4. Norigon says "Tonight's special: you." Phase 1 title card pops. I clear it with the conveyor trick the tip mentioned. Phase 2 starts, Norigon's ring turns red, Salmonster runs in; I KO Salmonster instead of fighting the big guy, the progress bar fills, a quip plays ("...You fight like a coupon."). I lose phase 3 once and retry only phase 3, not the whole fight.
- **Rewards / progression hooks:** boss medals (3 per boss), a unique "boss victory" colour style per boss (3 + 3 + 1 = 7 new colour styles: the Gallery shows 14 of 19 styles still locked so there is room), and a boss rematch in the Gauntlet's every-4th-wave slot that reuses the same phases (links the new Gauntlet course bosses to the Story bosses).
- **Risks and edge cases:** more authoring work per boss (rules + tip text + quips). Phase rules must not break Online or Party: keep them Story-only. Short-attention players on phones need the progress bar and a Skip Phase Cutscene. Hard to balance with 1 kaiju choice, so tune against a median kaiju (Gobzilla) and let the Helping Hand (belly boost) also lower boss phase length.
- **Size:** L (game rules for phase scripting, 7+ boss definitions, phase card UI, map node medals, defeat card text). Touches Story, results card, Gauntlet course bosses.
- **Score impact for me:** +1.2. Bosses would finally feel like the climax of a course and give the lost-3-times experience a learn-and-retry loop.

### Proposal 2: A Recurring Cast, Rival Rapport and a "Kitchen Tales" Hub (story that remembers me)
- **Problem it solves:** I met Crabbage, Squidgeon, Bloblin, Mothball, Toastbat, Norigon, Salmonster, Bubblegumbo, Chocomunch and each said one line and vanished. The rival lines do react to *how* I won (good) but not to *who* I am, which kaiju I play, or what I did before. Nothing connects the opening cutscene (Golden Spoon on a pedestal) to chapters 1-5. Second Helpings and Dessert are unsignposted. The title "NEXT UP" line (shot at title: "Clear Story ch 4" while I had 0% and "ch 6" while chapter 5 was uncleared) shows the story is tracked as milestones, not as a narrative.
- **How it would work (detailed design):**
  - Add a **Rapport** value per rival (0-5 hearts, shown on the rival tile in the right-hand chapter panel and in the Gallery card next to Mastery). Rapport +1 for each distinct way I beat them: Cutlery KO, KAIJU squash KO, Fall (shove), Giant Hand assist, or a flawless win (5 "styles" = 5 hearts) and 1 heart for losing 3 times (pity bond). Defeat/clear cards show a rival heart pulse ("+1 Crabbage rapport: shove style").
  - Chapter structure: after chapters 3, 6, 9 insert a **Kitchen Tale** interlude (a 30-45 s dialogue scene in the existing SCENE system, two rivals talking about the Golden Spoon with 2-3 lines of choice for the player: "Share the spoon" / "Keep the spoon" / "Joke around"). The choice only changes (a) which line the next boss opens with and (b) which of two epilogue variants appears in the Dessert Course. Choices are shown on a **Story Journal** page (title screen -> STORY -> new JOURNAL button next to REWARDS) so I can re-read scenes (the existing OPENING/SCENE buttons are the seed of this).
  - Rapport payoffs: at 3 hearts a rival cameos in Free Play as a one-line banter and at 5 hearts they give a Story-only trophy (a "recipe card": a tiny colour style or a table decoration). The Gallery gets a Journal tab showing each rival's relationship line ("Crabbage: respects your shove").
  - Make Second Helpings and Dessert Course reachable with a visible "NEXT BOOK" tile at the end of the map (chapter 13 node) and show locked state from the start so the arc is promised.
  - Final-boss foreshadowing (new this round) tied to rapport: with 3 or more total hearts, the foreshadowing line becomes personal ("Crabbage warned me about you").
- **Example moment:** After clearing Chapter 3, the Tale shows Toastbat and Mothball arguing over crumbs; I pick "Joke around". Chapter 4 intro: Norigon opens with "Heard you made the moths laugh." My Journal records the choice, Mothball rapport goes to 2 hearts, and the Gallery shows his heart-count.
- **Rewards / progression hooks:** Rapport hearts (17 rivals x 5 = 85 collectables), Story Journal that fills as I replay, rapport trophies, rapport-flavoured final-boss lines, 3 tale chapters with 3 variants each as a reason to replay on a second save slot or a different kaiju.
- **Risks and edge cases:** writing cost (about 60-80 short lines), localisation of lines, avoiding gating the story behind rapport (never block progress; hearts are cosmetic/flavour). Keep text short for kids and for text-box overruns (chapter-clear card already fits in shot-5 but has no spare room). Choices must not need a controller-friendly third button: use the existing 2-3 option button layout.
- **Size:** M-L (content writing, a small save field per rival, a Journal screen, a Tale scene type). Touches Story map, Gallery card, results card, Records.
- **Score impact for me:** +1.0. This is the missing "it is a story, not a ladder" feeling: recurring characters who remember how I played.

### Proposal 3: Story-Linked Economy and a Real Difficulty Dial ("Chef's Notes" + Story Pantry)
- **Problem it solves:** Token payouts in my session: ch1 win +17, ch2 flawless +44, ch3 +22, ch4 boss loss +58, ch4 loss +8, ch5 loss +15, ch5 5-round win +183. Rewards scale with match length, not with difficulty, result or first clears, so the best way to earn tokens is long, scrappy games, and a first-time flawless boss clear pays about as much as a loss. The Pantry has only 6 items (shot-24) and nothing story-related, Mastery levels (Rookie -> Line Cook -> Sous Chef -> Head Chef, Gallery shot-27) show a number but I never saw a Mastery reward. Story difficulty is fixed per chapter, so the ramp felt uneven: chapters 1-3 were walkovers with a basic attack, then chapter 4 (boss) spiked, and chapter 5 normal bots beat me once and a hard bot beat them: it is unclear what "normal" and "hard" mean in the story.
- **How it would work (detailed design):**
  - **First-clear payout table**: first clear of a chapter = 100 tokens x chapter number tier (ch1-4 = 100, ch5-8 = 150, ch9-12 = 200), boss first clear = +150, 3-star first time = +50. Replays pay a flat 15 (+5 per KO, cap 40), losses pay 0 beyond a flat 5 "participation". This replaces length-based earnings in Story only (Free Play can keep its current rule).
  - **Story Pantry** (new tab in the PANTRY and on the Story map): Recipe Cards sold for tokens: "Chef's Notes" (a one-line scouting hint for each chapter: 40 tokens, shows rival weaknesses before the chapter), "Spare Spoon" (start a Story match with one extra throwable, 60), "Second Plate" (one free Story retry that does not reset 3-star, 120, once per chapter), and a "Golden Apron" cosmetic for each Mastery tier.
  - **Story Difficulty dial** on the chapter panel (right side, under the star legend): Cosy (bots one tier easier, 2 stars max shown as "Cosy ★★"), Standard (current), Chef's Special (bots one tier harder, +50% tokens, shows Spicy-style red frame). This unifies with the existing "SPICY: clear it first" toggle (shot-2) so Spicy becomes the top rung of one dial rather than a separate locked toggle on the same screen.
  - **Mastery rewards table** (shown on the Gallery card under the Mastery line): Lv2 = a banter line, Lv4 = a colour style for that kaiju, Lv6 = a Story-only taunt voice line, Lv8 = a Mastery crown on the Story map node of their chapters. Today Mastery shows "Lv 4 Head Chef (36/240 XP)" with no preview of what Lv5 gives.
  - The "mercy" **Helping Hand** button (appeared after 2 losses, shot-17) must say what it does (+belly at round start? opponents weaker?) and show a cost-free note "no stars lost" or "caps at 2 stars" so it feels like a story option, not a hidden cheat.
- **Example moment:** I clear Chapter 4 for the first time and see "+250 Chef Tokens: FIRST CLEAR BOSS BONUS!". At the Pantry I buy Chef's Notes for Chapter 8 (40) and a Spare Spoon (60). On the chapter 8 panel I pick Standard. A loss pays +5, so I do not farm; I try Cosy for my younger sibling's turn.
- **Rewards / progression hooks:** predictable, readable token rewards, Mastery milestones with named rewards, a Difficulty dial that increases replay value (Cosy/Standard/Chef's Special x 12 chapters = up to 36 star conditions, which matches the existing 36 stars), and Story-specific token sinks.
- **Risks and edge cases:** a flat first-clear table means a one-time economy: make sure there is still a late-game token sink (Pantry has 6 items; add rotating weekly stock). Cosy must not hand out 3 stars for free: cap Cosy at 2 stars. Online/Party must keep the old length-based earnings. Avoid exposing a token farm: replays capped at 40 per match, daily soft cap of 300 in Story.
- **Size:** M (reward formula, Pantry tabs, difficulty dial UI, Mastery rewards table). Touches results receipt, Pantry, Gallery, Story map, Settings (COMEBACK HELP option text).
- **Score impact for me:** +0.8. A fair, readable difficulty/reward ramp is the other half of the Story Fan's "is the difficulty curve fair?" question.

## How the new features from this round felt
- **Chef Tokens / receipt card:** The receipt on the defeat results screen (shot-16: tokens, mastery, "Knocked out by: a KAIJU x2, cutlery", tip) is good and readable. But the story CHAPTER CLEAR card shows only a one-line "+17 Chef Tokens (17) · Mastery Lv 1 Rookie · 39/60 XP" and no "how you were knocked out"; and the first defeat card in the Story (shot-15) had no receipt at all while later ones did. The payout size feels random (see Proposal 3).
- **Kaiju Mastery:** The Gallery shows "Mastery Lv 4 Head Chef (36/240 XP)" but never what the next level unlocks. Needs a reward ladder (Proposal 3).
- **Weekly Orders / Pantry:** Three orders at 40 tokens each is a good light hook, and the progress bars read well (shot-24). The shop (6 items) is thin: three colours cost 120-180 tokens but I earned 413 tokens in 11 matches, so everything is affordable by about match 15 and then tokens have no sink.
- **Chef Rank:** "Dishwasher" in Records and in the Pantry. No visible ladder or next-rank threshold, so it is a label and not a goal.
- **Comeback help / mercy tip:** The Helping Hand button after 2 losses (shot-17) is the right idea for a story fan, but it has no explanation, and I took it without knowing whether it costs stars. Round 1 of the Helping Hand match started with a visibly easier fight but never told me why.
- **"What beat you" on defeat:** Very good. Changed correctly between KAIJU squash, Giant Hand and cutlery. Making it appear on the clear card for a hard-won win would also help.
- **Rivals react to how you won:** Works (flawless vs close lines), but the suffix "...That was close, though. Next time!" was appended even after a 5-knockout boss win (Norigon, 2 rounds to 1) and after a 1-star clear (Mothball). It reads as a template tail instead of an actual reaction.
- **Retry after losing a Dessert course / final-boss foreshadowing:** I did not reach these. Cannot judge.
- **Giant Hand explains itself:** The Giant Hand banner ("The giant hand goes for whoever has the fullest belly.") is shown as a round-start tip and in the defeat card; I still died to it twice because my "attack" loop never stomped. The tip arrives as tiny text during the countdown, which is easy to miss.
- **Daily medals / Gauntlet / House Rules / Clarity (KAIJU rings):** Not touched (Story-focused profile); I saw the red stomp ring on Norigon (shot-16) and it reads clearly.
- **Text fit:** see Bugs.

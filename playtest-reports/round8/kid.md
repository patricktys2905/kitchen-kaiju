## What worked for me
- **The art and humour are the best thing here.** Crabbage with a lettuce wig screaming on a roast chicken (shot-12), the angry steaming Dumplord who "won" the Daily (shot-48), the pink Bubblegumbo and the strawberry Gobzilla roaring at the end of every match, "Not even ONE round for me?!" from every beaten boss. A 9-year-old laughs at all of it.
- **KAIJU mode is hugely satisfying.** The huge strawberry with the rings (shot-24) and squashing rivals is the moment a kid will repeat to a friend.
- **HELPING HAND is a fantastic idea** for me: it appears on the DEFEATED screen (shot-22), gives a full belly plus a Mustard Shield, and I won the retry straight away. It is the best anti-frustration tool in the game.
- **Kid Mode in Story** really does make rivals "easy", chats are 2 lines, the DEFEATED screen text is bigger ("So close! You took a round off them." is kind, shot-22).
- **"▼ YOU" marker** above my kaiju in Hot Dumpling (shot-46) made me find myself instantly.
- **TWIST picker** (shot-34) is a joke machine: BOUNCY CASTLE "nobody falls off", FISTS ONLY, MINI KAIJU + BOUNCY CASTLE. Perfect for a kid.
- **Table Workshop** (shot-59/60) is fun and clear: big icon palette, UNDO works, Giant hand label appears on the preview.
- **New-kaiju reveal** (shot-70) with the confetti and "Playable now in Free Play..." was lovely.
- **Chapter clear receipts** carry the First Bite / Lime colour reward: the "you got a colour!" hook works on me.
- **Quick tips** under the countdown are useful and short ("Mustard Shield blocks one hit, but not a fall off the table.").
- **Move Lab** is a great kid toy: STOMP, then THROW/SWIPE, Esc leaves.

## 🧾 Exactly what would make this a 9/10 for me
1. **Make Kid Mode a real, visible mode everywhere (+0.8).**
   - *What:* When KID MODE is ON, bots are EASY in Story, Free Play, Party, Daily AND Party Cup, and every set-up screen says so.
   - *Where:* Free Play set-up (shot-26: "Bot difficulty: NORMAL", "Bot: NORMAL (tap)" under each bot), Party set-up (shot-38), Daily result (shot-48: "Tough bots? Set BOT LEVEL to EASY").
   - *How:* the labels must read "Bot difficulty: EASY (Kid Mode)" and show a small 🧒 KID MODE badge on the title, set-up screens and the pause menu. Kid-mode easy bots must visibly be easy: no bot goes KAIJU before round 2, bots throw at 50% accuracy, and a bot never KOs me in the first 8 seconds of a round. In my Free Play match (shot-27 to shot-32) Gobzilla bot won 2 rounds in a row and chained KAIJU, "DOUBLE SKEWER" and a blender KO against me while the screen still said NORMAL.
2. **Shorter matches for kids (+0.7).**
   - *What:* Free Play/Party "FIRST TO 3" ran **7 rounds** (shot-30, 31) because 4 players trade wins. Kid Mode should default to FIRST TO 2 and a round timer cap (see Proposal 1).
   - *How:* Kid Mode: first-to-2 in Free Play/Party, Party Cup defaults to 3 games (it defaults to 6, shot-38/39), Story chapters already first-to-2. A 9-year-old match should end in under 3 minutes.
3. **Result screens in 3 lines, not a receipt (+0.6).**
   - *What:* After a match I get a 9-line "🧾 THIS MATCH" box (shot-32, 48) with coins, XP, mastery, two orders, two achievements, a "Knocked out by" list and two tips. A kid reads none of it.
   - *How:* Kid Mode shows one big line "You got 🪙 +183 and a new colour: Lava!" plus a "MORE ▾" button for the rest. Tips only after a loss and only one. Also hide the keyboard line "1 / START rematch · Esc menu" when the REMATCH/MENU buttons are on screen.
4. **Ask before spending my coins (+0.5).** I tapped ✨ SHINY in the Gallery and 300 🪙 vanished with no price on the button, no "are you sure", no preview (shot-52 then Pantry shot-55 showed 324 left of 599+). Then one tap on Toffee in the Pantry took 40 🪙 silently (shot-56). A kid will buy the wrong thing. Add "Buy ✨ SHINY for 300 🪙? YES / NO" with a preview of the shiny look, and put the price on the button.
5. **Fix the unfinishable Practice and the stale NEXT UP (+0.4).** Practice stuck at "7/8 SWIPE up close F / X / SWIPE" (shot-6, shot-7) even after I swiped next to the dummy rival with F and X; the title still shows "Finish the 1-minute Practice (+20 🪙)" and the "NEW HERE? 1-MINUTE PRACTICE" button disappeared after I quit once. The first-minute tutorial must complete, or a Skip must count it.
6. **Declutter the screen-edge pile of popups (+0.3).** shot-28 shows SEED SPRAY! / UNSTOPPABLE APPETITE! / ON A ROLL! / STOMP READY! / KAIJU! / DOUBLE SKEWER! stacked and overlapping on the left, plus the INSTANT REPLAY and FAST FORWARD banners stacked. Show max 2 popups at a time, queue the rest.
7. **Do not show "FAILED" or "DEFEATED" in big red to a kid (+0.3).** shot-48 "DAILY CHALLENGE FAILED" and "DEFEATED" (shot-22) are harsh. In Kid Mode use "TRY AGAIN TOMORROW!" / "SO CLOSE!" with the bronze medal first.
8. **Explain each party game in one picture before it starts (+0.3).** Daily dropped me into Hot Dumpling with only a countdown and a tip about swiping (shot-45). First-time rules card: "Don't hold the 🥟 when it pops!" with a 3-second animation.
9. **Make the loss tip match the loss (+0.2).** In Chapter 2 I was KO'd by a KAIJU squash ("YOUknockedoutbySQUIDGEON(squash)") but the DEFEATED text said "💡 Thrown cutlery got you!" (shot-22).
10. **Bigger, fewer words on Help, Trials and the Pantry (+0.3).** Help is ~14px wall text (shot-58), Trials rows are 12-14px (shot-50), Chef Rank text in the Pantry is a different, smaller bold style (shot-55). Kid Mode should switch Help to an illustrated one-screen cheat sheet.
11. **Show what ✨ SHINY looks like (+0.2).** After buying, the portrait in the Gallery and the KAIJU preview in Move Lab (shot-54) looked identical to the normal form. I paid 300 🪙 and could not see the difference.
12. **Polish items from the issue table below (minor/polish) (+0.3).**

If 1 to 5 are done I would give 8.5; with 6 to 11 as well, 9+.

## The single biggest structural problem
**Kid Mode is only a Story setting.** The player (a 9-year-old) who picks the game up on a couch gets the friendly mode in Story, but the part of the game kids actually play with family, Free Play, Party Modes and Daily, still has NORMAL bots, 7-round matches and nine-line receipts. In Free Play (shot-26 to shot-32) every screen said NORMAL, I lost the first four rounds to a Gobzilla bot, and the match took longer than a kid's attention span. When I lose repeatedly there is a good tool (HELPING HAND), but it only appears after a Story defeat. So the strongest anti-frustration feature is hidden in the mode where I already have the easiest bots.

The second big problem is **reading**: the result screens, Help, Trials and the title are text-first. The game's humour and art are strong; a kid wants to see "YOU WON! 🪙 +183, new colour Lava!" and tap REMATCH.

## Big change proposals (2–3, most important first)
### Proposal 1: Real "Kid Mode" across every mode
- **Problem it solves:** Kid Mode is invisible and Story-only; kids lose and read too much in Free Play, Party and Daily (issues 1-3, 12-14, 40).
- **How it would work (detailed design):**
  - Settings: KID MODE moves to the top, labelled "🧒 KID MODE: easy bots, short games, big words".
  - When ON, on the title screen a small 🧒 badge shows, and every set-up screen shows "Bots: EASY (Kid Mode)".
  - Free Play/Party/Cup defaults: FIRST TO 2, Party Cup 3 games, rounds capped at 45 s (sudden death: whoever stands nearest the middle wins). Bots: EASY plus no KAIJU before round 2, 50% throw accuracy, never target the same kaiju twice in a row.
  - After 2 lost rounds in a row, the HELPING HAND (full belly + Mustard Shield) is offered between rounds as a big green button, not only after a Story defeat.
  - Result screen: one headline ("YOU WON!" / "SO CLOSE!"), one reward line with the biggest thing earned, a REMATCH button and a "MORE ▾" drawer for the receipt, standings and tips.
  - Replace "FAILED" / "DEFEATED" with "TRY AGAIN" tones.
- **Example moment:** I lose round 1 and 2 in Free Play. A banner says "Need a boost? 🍀 HELPING HAND" and I tap it; round 3 I start with full belly and a shield, go KAIJU, squash two bots, win the round, laugh.
- **Rewards / progression hooks:** Kid Mode still pays coins and mastery (maybe ×1.0), with a "Brave Bite" sticker for each win without the helping hand.
- **Risks and edge cases:** Older siblings want normal bots: let Free Play seats have a per-seat bot level (already exists) and show Kid Mode only changing defaults; make sure Daily stays comparable (Kid Mode Daily gets a separate medal row or no streak penalty).
- **Size:** M
- **Score impact for me:** +1.0

### Proposal 2: "Spend safely" — shop confirmations and previews
- **Problem it solves:** I spent 300 🪙 on Shiny and 40 🪙 on Toffee with one tap each, and I could not see what Shiny looks like (issues 4, 5, 45, 46, 52, 54).
- **How it would work (detailed design):**
  - Every purchase (Gallery Shiny, Pantry colours, Lunchbox, Lifeline) opens a small card: big preview of the item on my kaiju, "BUY for 300 🪙?", YES (green) / NO (red) buttons, and "You will have 299 🪙 left".
  - Shiny button states: "✨ SHINY · 300 🪙" / "✨ OWNED · TAP TO WEAR" / "✨ WEARING ✓".
  - Cannot-afford items show a "save up" bar ("76 more 🪙").
  - In Kid Mode, purchases over 100 🪙 also need a tap-and-hold for 1 second (the "grown-up check").
- **Example moment:** I tap SHINY for Gobzilla, the card shows a sparkly Gobzilla spinning, I tap YES, the confetti fires and the Gallery portrait sparkles.
- **Rewards / progression hooks:** A "First purchase!" sticker; a "wardrobe" row on the title that shows what I am wearing.
- **Risks and edge cases:** extra tap for adults (acceptable); cosmetics previews need render support for each of 20 kaiju x 21 colours (use a single preview sprite shader).
- **Size:** S
- **Score impact for me:** +0.5

### Proposal 3: Picture-first results, help and rules (no walls of text)
- **Problem it solves:** Receipts, Help, Trials and tips are all dense small text (issues 14-16, 37, 41, 56, 58, 60).
- **How it would work (detailed design):**

- **Example moment:** In my first Daily, a card pops up: a little dumpling ticking, "Pass the 🥟 before it pops!", and I get it before the countdown ends.
- **Rewards / progression hooks:** A "Learned all the rules" sticker after seeing every mode card.

# Playtest evaluation — newbie (The First-Timer)

**Fun score: 7 / 10** (charming, instantly readable and the practice is excellent; but my first matches were over in seconds and I never felt I was playing, just being carried by auto-attack)

## What I did
- Title screen -> HELP (Basics + Cutlery tabs) -> 1-minute Practice (all 8 steps) -> Free Play setup -> 2-player match (won 5-1) -> 5-player match (partially, lost round 1 by squash, saw a bot win round 2).
- About 40 calls, /errors: none.

## What matched my goals / what was fun
- Title art is lovely and the "NEW HERE? 1-MINUTE PRACTICE" button is exactly what I needed, in the middle of the screen.
- Practice teaches throw, pickup, stomp/KAIJU, swipe, dash step by step with a hint bar. Finishing it felt good and "All done" is clear.
- Going KAIJU (giant strawberry dino) is a great payoff moment. Round-end banners and "TOASTBAT shoved BLOBLIN off the table" captions are funny and clear.
- Free Play setup is easy: click a seat, click PLAY. Match-end screen with stats is nice.

## What took too long / was tedious
- Nothing really tedious; the practice dummy just stands still, so steps 1-3 feel slow.

## What was over too quickly / too shallow
- 2-player normal bot rounds ended in ~4-10 seconds; five rounds in under a minute, a bot that dies to the first throw. Felt trivial.
- Matches with 5 players ended chaotically fast, I got squashed by a KAIJU-mode bot with no idea how to avoid it.

## Confusing or unclear
- Free Play: clicking only my seat then PLAY quietly adds 1 bot; the seats screen text says "click empty seats to add bots" but I did not realise PLAY would fill in. OK but unclear.
- Practice: ending says "Esc -> pause -> QUIT" and QUIT dropped me into the Help screen, not title; slightly odd.
- Knife has very short range; at distance nothing seemed to happen when I threw (no range indicator told me why).
- Fork/knife/pizza cutter icons are small, hard to tell what I hold mid-fight. Practice hint "KO'd by YOU" wording about my own KO was ambiguous.
- Bottom-left hint bar text is small.

## Bugs found
- No JS errors (/errors: none).
- Possible: in practice, walking right at y~380 stopped at x=557 (blocked by the gravy boat area); unclear if intended. Move at 1,0 from centre stalled until I went around. Not obviously communicated.

## Top 3 changes that would raise my score
1. Make Normal bots tougher/smarter or rounds longer so early matches are not decided in seconds.
2. Show clearer feedback on throw range/charge and a bigger held-weapon indicator, plus a warning when a KAIJU-mode rival is about to stomp.
3. Make Free Play seat setup obvious (auto-add bots with a visible default, "Quit" in practice returning to title).

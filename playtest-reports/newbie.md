# Playtest evaluation — The First-Timer

**Fun score: 7 / 10** (instantly charming and readable, with a great tutorial; but my first real match was over in about 30 seconds with little feedback, and a few menu labels confused me)

## What I did
- Title screen -> HELP (Basics, Cutlery tabs) -> PRACTICE (guided 8-step tutorial, finished 7/8 before leaving; the SWIPE step never ticked for me)
- Free Play: joined with SPACE, played a Normal-difficulty 1v1 to the end of a match (first to 5, won 5-1), saw the winner screen
- Records screen (stats and achievements updated, "First Bite" unlocked)
- Story chapter 1 (opening cutscene, then a match vs Crabbage; I lost 1-2 and got a DEFEATED screen with RETRY/MAP)
- About 80 calls, 20 screenshots checked. /errors: none.

## What matched my goals / what was fun
- Title screen is gorgeous and tells me the tone at once. Six big colour-coded mode buttons, and Story is highlighted first.
- The How to Play text is short and clear (goal, throw, swipe, dash, stomp, controls). The cutlery tab with icons and one-line stats is lovely.
- The Practice tutorial is the best thing: a checklist panel that ticks off steps, a harmless dummy, and it teaches belly -> STOMP -> KAIJU. Going giant is a great "wow" moment.
- A pink dotted aim line shows where a throw goes, and short on-screen text lines say what happened ("GOBZILLA skewered SQUIDGEON").
- The "Instant replay" with the giant hand sweeping the table showed me why I died. It made the loss feel funny, not unfair.
- Match end screen has fun awards (Crumb Monster, Butterfingers) and a rematch hint. The story defeat screen is kind ("So close!") and has Retry.
- Rounds are quick (5-15 s), so it stays snappy.

## What took too long / was tedious
- Some rounds stall: a 40+ second standoff where both sides held cutlery and neither closed in.
- Free Play defaults to "First to 99 rounds", which would be endless. I had to find R to change it to 3/5.

## What was over too quickly / too shallow
- A whole easy match took about 30 s of play; the first Free Play round ended before I felt I had done anything.
- Free Play 1v1 on one table, with power-ups and hazards OFF by default, feels thin. The fun options are hidden behind letter keys.

## Confusing or unclear
- Free Play setup: clicking an empty "THROW to join" slot does nothing with the mouse; you must press the throw key (SPACE). A mouse/touch first-timer would stall here.
- The footer key "2 bots" actually cycled bot difficulty (Easy -> Normal) on my press. Label and result don't match. Bots are added with B/N, not 2.
- "First to 99 rounds" reads strangely, and the score reads "0/99".
- Pressing Esc on the match-end screen dropped me to the setup screen with no confirmation, and Esc mid-match went straight to the menu without a pause or quit prompt.
- Practice: I swiped with F and the step never ticked ("SWIPE up close" stayed at 7/8). Possibly needs to hit the dummy.
- Chapter 1 "easy" Crabbage beat me. A very first story match probably wants gentler handling or a hint on dashing and swiping.
- The Story cutscene dialogue box overlapped the map UI; text appeared cut off ("Rule ever") mid-typewriter, which was fine, but there is a lot of tap-to-continue.
- The setup screen is a wall of keyboard hints (about 40 labels) that a newcomer won't read.

## Bugs found
- Free Play: empty-slot click does nothing (could be intended, but it is a poor first experience).
- Practice SWIPE step could not be completed via F key press in the harness (the key may need the dummy in range; unsure whether this is a real bug).
- Footer "2 bots" key label vs. actual behaviour (changes difficulty).
- /errors was empty.

## Top 3 changes that would raise my score
1. Make Free Play setup mouse/touch friendly (click a slot to join, labelled buttons for difficulty, rounds, bots, with a clear big PLAY button) and default to first-to-3 or 5, not 99.
2. After Practice, push me straight into an easy first match with short contextual hints, and soften chapter 1 (slower or weaker Crabbage) so a first-timer wins their first story fight.
3. Add a pause/quit confirm on Esc and fix the stalled-standoff rounds (for example a shrinking table or a hurry-up hazard) so rounds never drag on for 40+ seconds.

# Playtest evaluation: The Story Fan

**Fun score: 6.5 / 10** (Charming cast and a clean 12-chapter spine, but the story is told in single-line barks, bosses are scary on paper and soft in practice, and the difficulty curve has a spike at ch5 and a plateau afterwards.)

## What I did
- Fresh save on the title screen. Opened Story and played every chapter of The Grand Feast in order (ch 1 to 12), reading all pre-match dialogue, tips and clear screens. Then started Second Helpings ch 1 (won) and Dessert Course 1/4 (lost, no defeat screen shown).
- Matches played: about 17. Chapters 1-4 and 6-11 with my seat on `auto:normal` or `auto:hard` (I did hand-drive the first round of ch1 and part of ch4 to see the stomp coach and the boss). Retries: ch4 (1 loss, helped by nothing, won on retry), ch5 (3 losses, Helping Hand then won), ch12 (2 losses, Helping Hand then won).
- Looked at: the opening scene, the chapter map, the Story Rewards screen, the Defeated modal (including HELPING HAND), unlock popups (Minturtle, Freezer Shelf, Pengwhip, Candy Shop, Velvaroo + Chef's Nightmare), the ending scene.
- About 90 harness commands, roughly 35 minutes of game time. `/errors` at start, middle, end: none.

## What worked for me (briefly)
- The cast is genuinely charming, and the callbacks land: Bloblin giggles "Hehe. Wobble wobble." in ch2 and returns in the ch8 boss as "You laughed at my wobble. Now you'll SLIDE."; Crabbage "Remember me? I've been practising my snips." in ch7; Norigon "I told you the feast gets hotter. I'm back, and I'm not alone." in ch11. Rivals who recur and escalate are exactly what makes a short story feel like a story.
- Every chapter has a distinct table gimmick taught by the chapter TIP (wind, cleaver lanes, burners, ant trail, turntable, toasters) so the campaign doubles as a tutorial for hazards.
- Chapter-clear lines per rival ("Ink-credible... We'll be back. Probably wobblier.", "I'm just going to sit in the freezer for a while.") and "Next course: X. Y is already sharpening the cutlery" is a nice breadcrumb.
- Rewards screen (shot-8) is great: every locked reward shows exactly what it takes and your progress ("4/8 cleared", "you have 9/30").
- HELPING HAND on the Defeated modal (shot-9) is a kind catch for a campaign player, and starting with STOMP READY made ch5 and ch12 winnable.
- The 3 boss chapters give a kaiju or table as a prize. Ending scene ("The Head Chef lifts the GOLDEN SPOON high... and hands it to you.") is a sweet button.

## The single biggest structural problem
**The campaign has no narrative momentum or stakes: it is 12 identical beats (one line of banter, a match, one line of banter) with a flat difficulty line, and the ending has nothing to say about the world you spent an evening in.** I wanted to follow a story. What I got was a series of cards: each chapter shows a table, one line from each rival, a tip, a match, one reply line and a "Next course" teaser. The characters are fun but they never talk to each other, never react to what actually happened in the match (my "5 knockouts!" line is the same template whether I shoved someone off in the last second or never fought), and your own kaiju never speaks at all. The plot ("Head Chef hosts the Grand Feast, Golden Spoon is the prize") is stated in the opening and then simply re-confirmed by Dumplord in ch12 ("The Golden Spoon stays with ME!") with no setup that Dumplord was the mastermind. Bosses at ch4/8/12 have good intro lines but are mechanically just "starts round with stomp ready", and as a hard-bot stand-in I beat Norigon (ch4) and Bloblin (ch8) three rounds to zero. The difficulty ramps once (ch5, first to 3, two normal bots: I lost 3 times in a row, then a flat plateau where chapters 6 to 11 fell 3-1 or 3-0 with no incident), so there is no rising tension either.

It matters for a Story fan because the story is the reason to play this mode over Free Play: if the mode is just "Free Play against named bots with a unlock checklist", the dialogue is wasted on someone who skips it, and a fan feels nothing at the finish.

## Big change proposals (2-3, most important first)

### Proposal 1: "The Feast Has a Plot": story beats, reactive banter and a real finale arc
- **Problem it solves:** Every chapter is the same one-line-banter-then-match; the villain reveal in ch12 is unforeshadowed; clear-screen text is a template ("N knockouts! The whole kitchen is talking."); the player kaiju is mute; the ending is 4 lines then "THE END" (see ch12 end sequence). A Story Fan needs a through-line and a reason to care about the Golden Spoon.
- **How it would work (detailed design):**
  - **Add a 3-act script with a small per-kaiju speaking cast.** Act 1 (ch1-4): "The invitation"; Act 2 (ch5-8): "Something is wrong in the kitchen" (the Head Chef is being quietly pressured; rivals mention a "Spoon Thief" and a mysterious Dumplord's note); Act 3 (ch9-12): "The coup" (Dumplord revealed as organising the boss chain; the bosses were all sworn to him).
  - **Cutscene shape:** keep the current 2-3 line dialogue box but add a **between-chapters scene** after each boss (ch4, ch8) of 6-8 lines with 2 or 3 speakers, a background change (e.g. Head Chef at the pass, a silhouette of Dumplord in a window) and one **player choice** (see below). After ch8 and ch12 a 12-line scene. Total new text: about 150 lines, 3 new scene backdrops (can reuse table art with a vignette).
  - **Reactive clear lines:** the clear screen picks the rival line by *how the match went* using data already in the match result: lost a round? ("Close one!"); won 3-0 ("Flawless" line); last KO was a shove / cutlery / KAIJU squash / hazard; you went KAIJU 0 times vs 3+ times; belly was full for over 10 s without stomping. Write 3 variants per rival (loss, narrow win, flawless) = 20 rivals x 3 = 60 lines. The existing generic "You went KAIJU 2 times" line stays but moves to a stats row.
  - **Player voice and choice:** each time you pick your kaiju the game uses that kaiju's one-liner in dialogue ("GOBZILLA: *chomp*") and offers a **pick-1-of-3 reply** in 4 chapters (ch3, ch6, ch9, ch11): Brave / Cheeky / Kind. Replies do not change the plot but give a small colour tag on the chapter card and unlock a different rival reaction line and a different **epilogue card** (see Proposal 3). A reply picked costs 1 extra Space press, so it is short.
  - **Foreshadow the boss:** from ch5 onward the Dumplord silhouette appears in a 2-second pre-match overlay behind the rival intro ("Someone is watching from the pantry.") and Hotchog/Norigon lines mention him so ch12 reads as a reveal.
- **Example moment:** Ch8 "The Big Melt". After you drop Bloblin the usual "I'm just going to sit in the freezer for a while" plays, then a new 7-line scene: Mothball whispers "He said we would get the Spoon if we stopped you." Head Chef, off-screen: "Who said?" Bloblin: "...Dumplord." A pick-1: [Brave] "Then I'll ask him myself." [Cheeky] "Is he paying overtime?" [Kind] "Come with me, we'll stop him together." Next chapter's rival banter references your pick ("Heard you want to ask Dumplord yourself? Ha!").
- **Rewards / progression hooks:** Reply choices unlock a **Story Journal** (a new tab in the Story map, 12 pages) that shows a rival portrait and the line they said; collecting all 3 reply variants for a chapter is a new "Story Fan" achievement row (12 chapters x 3). Replaying chapters becomes meaningful (go back and pick the other reply for a different rival line, which also compounds with the 3-star drive).
- **Risks and edge cases:** Text overload for kids; keep every scene skippable with the existing SKIP CHAT (S) button and remember the "seen" state per scene so replays and retries do not replay a 12-line scene (retry currently correctly skips dialogue, keep it). Localisation size. Online/Party players never see any of it, so no cost there. Keep reactive lines out of retries to avoid repetition.
- **Size:** M (writing-heavy, data-driven). Touches the story chapter data, the clear screen, the dialogue component and a new Journal list screen. No engine changes.
- **Score impact for me:** +1.0. This single change turns the campaign from a checklist into a story I want to see the end of, and it is the biggest delta for this profile.

### Proposal 2: A real difficulty curve and boss design (boss phases, per-chapter scaling and "Story Assist" that is not just a one-time stomp)
- **Problem it solves:** I lost ch5 three times (first to 3, two normal bots, round after round) then won ch6 to ch11 with no pressure; the three bosses were the easiest "boss" content I saw (3-0 for Norigon retry, 3-0 Bloblin) while ch5 was the hardest chapter of the first 11. The only bosses mechanic is "starts every round with stomp ready", the same for Norigon, Bloblin and Dumplord (ch12 "Everything you've learned, now!" is the same stomp-ready plus a hazard). Second Helpings ch1 drops back to easy bots (Sloba easy, Avocadrake easy), which is fine as a reset but gives no sense of a harder game. Defeat gives only a quote and Helping Hand.
- **How it would work (detailed design):**
  - **A chapter difficulty table** (replace the ad-hoc "easy/normal/hard" labels): each chapter gets a target "pressure" value 1-10 that is summed from (a) bot level per seat, (b) number of rivals, (c) first-to-N, (d) hazard intensity. Proposed Grand Feast curve: ch1 1, ch2 2, ch3 2, ch4 (boss) 4, ch5 3 (currently feels like 5; drop to first-to-2 or one normal+one easy), ch6 4, ch7 5, ch8 (boss) 6, ch9 5, ch10 6, ch11 7, ch12 (boss) 9. Second Helpings starts at 5 and rises to 10. Dessert 8 to 10. So the player never faces a *harder* chapter than the boss they just beat, and each act has a dip then a spike.
  - **Boss phases (3 bosses, 3 distinct designs, not just "stomp ready"):**
    - Norigon (ch4): Phase 1 normal bot with stomp-ready start. At 1 round left (his round score is 1), he summons 2 sushi minions on the conveyor (use existing bot spawn, 6 s lifetime, 1 hit KO). Telegraph: belt speeds up, "NORIGON: ROLL OUT!" banner.
    - Bloblin (ch8): Leaves slippery puddles when he stomps (existing puddle hazard), and splits into 2 half-size blobs at round 3 (each KO is half score). Telegraph: ring on the floor + wobble.
    - Dumplord (ch12): Three-round structure that is the finale: R1 he fights alone with toasters, R2 Hotchog and Pizzaraptor join (as now), R3 he "eats the Golden Spoon" and gets a permanent +25% size/KAIJU for 6 s every 20 s, with the ring warning (reuse the new KAIJU counterplay ring). Player gets the Spoon-grab as a visible objective (existing Golden Spoon mode art): grabbing it from the cake stand (positioned on the table edge) ends the final round instantly as a win.
  - **Tunable Helping Hand:** replace the single freebie ("start with STOMP READY") with a 3-step ladder that appears after each consecutive loss on the same chapter: loss 1 = tip text re-shown; loss 2 = Helping Hand (stomp ready); loss 3 = also "bots -1 level" for that chapter only with a visible chip "ASSIST ON", and the chapter's stars cap at 2 when ASSIST is on. Tell the player before they accept (the first Helping Hand I took was ch5 and it silently still awarded 3 stars: see "Bugs / notes").
  - **Retry flow:** Defeated modal gets a one-line "What beat you" summary from match data ("Chocomunch KOed you 3 times with chopsticks. Try the chopsticks lane in the wind.") so a loss teaches. After 2 losses suggest Free Play practice on that table with that rival ("Practise vs Chocomunch").
- **Example moment:** Ch12 R3 starts with Dumplord at belly 100 and the toasters ticking. A ring grows around his feet (the new stomp warning), you dash out, he lands, dizzy 0.75 s, you land a swipe, he roars, "THE SPOON IS MINE!", the Golden Spoon flashes on the cake stand. You vault the table edge, grab it, and the round ends with a camera push on the spoon, not a score pop. The epilogue plays.
- **Rewards / progression hooks:** Boss clear with no Assist awards a boss-specific **title card** (Spoon Taker, Belt Breaker, Melt Master) shown on the Story map and Records. A "Flawless boss" star (3-0) gets a unique colour style tied to that boss's palette (Norigon Rose, Bloblin Cyan). Gives achievers/story fans a clearer goal than "3 stars" alone.
- **Risks and edge cases:** More scripted boss logic equals more bugs: build it as data (a list of phase triggers) on the existing bot/round system. Phones/touch: boss minions must be 1-hit and readable. 1-player only: the Story is single player so there are no scaling issues. Boss and Assist must not leak into Free Play or Online.
- **Size:** L. Touches the story chapter data, bot spawner and round-end logic, a new boss-phase data format, the Defeated modal, Records/colour unlocks.
- **Score impact for me:** +0.8. The finale becomes the high point of the story, the ch5 wall goes away, and a loss becomes useful.

### Proposal 3: Postgame that is part of the story: "The Dessert Course" as a true epilogue with the Second Helpings and a visible 100% goal
- **Problem it solves:** After the ending ("You collected ★ 25/36. Go for all 3 stars, or brawl in Free Play!") the game offers four disconnected things: Second Helpings, Dessert 0/4, Spicy 0/12, 3-star replay. I opened Second Helpings and the first stage is just easy bots again with no sense of "this is the hard post-game" (ch1 Sloba easy, Avocadrake easy). Dessert 1/4 dropped me into a 4-seat match straight from the Story map: "DESSERT COURSE 1/4 · SUGAR CRASH", I lost the match (Chocomunch won) and the game returned me to the Grand Feast map with **no Defeated modal, no retry button, no Helping Hand**: that felt like the game dropped me. The Story page says "Story 61% complete" right after I finished the credits, which reads as a bug to a fan: the ending feels cheapened.
- **How it would work (detailed design):**
  - **Define "complete":** Grand Feast = 100% when 12 chapters cleared (that is the "story" bar and shows "THE END" after Dumplord), then the line becomes "Post-game: 61% of everything". Show two meters: STORY (clears) and MASTERY (stars + spicy + second helpings + dessert).
  - **A hub scene after the ending:** after "THE END", BACK TO MAP goes to a new **Kitchen Hub** map (a single illustrated screen with 4 doors: Second Helpings (the fridge), Dessert Course (the pantry), Spicy Mode (the stove), Replay Stars (the trophy shelf)). Each door is a card with a one-line "where you left off" and progress.
  - **Second Helpings as the real hard mode:** 6 chapters with the cleared kaiju's scaled-up counterparts: all rivals one tier higher (easy to normal, normal to hard), plus a **"Rematch" mutator per chapter** (existing twists: Big Heads, Slippery, etc.) so it is visibly not the first run again. First-to values match the first run for readability. Add 2 new lines of dialogue per chapter that reference the earlier run ("You beat me on the Freezer Shelf, but this is MY fridge.").
  - **Dessert Course as 4 mini-episodes with a named recurring sweet villain (e.g. Velvaroo, the new unlock):** each has the match, a short comic-strip 3-panel intro, and a consistent Defeated/Retry flow (fix the missing modal).
  - **Final reward:** clearing Dessert 4/4 unlocks the "Chef's Hat" colour for every kaiju and a **Credits-plus scene** where each rival you beat appears in a group photo around the table (compose it from the Gallery art). The photo includes the colour styles you currently have equipped.
- **Example moment:** Ending plays; "BACK TO MAP" opens the Hub. The stove has a small "!" because Spicy is available. The fridge shows "Second Helpings: new ranking, all rivals up a tier". I open Dessert, see Velvaroo and a 3-panel strip, lose, get the same Defeated modal and "Practise vs Velvaroo" button, and try again.
- **Rewards / progression hooks:** Mastery meter 0-100% with milestone rewards at 25/50/75/100 (a kaiju colour, a table, a title, the group photo). It gives an answer to "what do I do after the credits?" which is the core retention problem of a campaign.
- **Risks and edge cases:** Requires clear separation between Story clear and Mastery so achievers (who count stars) do not feel their percentage dropped. Need to ensure Dessert is unlockable by those who skip Second Helpings, or explain the gate ("Beat chapter 12 to unlock", which seemed to be the gate for me). Keep the Hub skippable on phones (one tap to the old map).
- **Size:** M to L. Touches the Story screen, adds a hub screen, edits Second Helpings data (bot tiers, mutators), the Dessert result flow and Records.
- **Score impact for me:** +0.7. It repairs the weakest moment of my playthrough (the post-credits cliff and the silent Dessert loss) and gives the fan an epilogue.

## How the new features from this round felt
- **STOMP coach:** In ch1 round 1 my belly went from 25 to 100 in about 4 s of `crumbs`; the "BELLY FULL! PRESS STOMP → KAIJU" prompt appeared over the kaiju (shot-3). It works and is clear, but in the same frame it overlapped the "STOMP READY!" banner (the red banner text is hidden behind the yellow one, see shot-3 near [700,530]). It also says "PRESS STOMP" in my keyboard-less harness, where the brief says "PRESS E": a text mismatch worth making device-aware. Bigger version: a 5-second guided prompt for the first KAIJU in ch1 that freezes the action once (slow-mo) so a first-time Story player does not miss it.
- **KAIJU counterplay (ring before stomp, flashing ring before shrink, 0.75 s dizzy):** I was squashed by Norigon in ch4 (`YOU knocked out by NORIGON (squash)`) after he "went KAIJU" within about 4 s of round start, which happened in two of three rounds, so the ring helps only if you are already watching him. For a Story boss the counterplay should be teachable: a one-time tutorial moment in ch4's pre-match ("Watch the red ring: step out!") and a boss callout banner. I did not feel the dizzy window in bot play, so I cannot say it is balanced.
- **Gallery progress ("you have 9/30 stars", "4/12 cleared"):** Excellent on the Story Rewards screen (shot-8), where it is a real goal list; it makes story rewards a roadmap. The mystery-kaiju rows are great teasers. A bigger version: tap a locked row to jump to the chapter.
- **Gauntlet lives / Party Golden Spoon / Cup length:** not touched by my profile.
- **Helping Hand (defeat modal):** Worked, but it silently kept 3 stars (ch5 and ch6 clears show full stars) which feels like the assist is free; and I never saw it offered on the first loss, only after 2-3 losses (ch5 offered after the 3rd loss, ch12 after 2nd). Rules should be visible.

## Bugs that block play
- **No Defeated modal after losing Dessert 1/4.** Steps: finish Grand Feast, Story screen, tap `🍰 DESSERT 0/4` (880,50) (a match starts immediately), lose the match to Chocomunch (`MATCH OVER (winner: CHOCOMUNCH)`). The game returns to the Grand Feast map with nothing shown (shot-16); no retry, no result. `/errors`: none.
- **Story screen reached a match without confirmation.** Steps: from the Story map after ch12 clear, `Space` through the ending, then another `Space` starts "Round 1 · Breakfast Rush" (shot-12) instead of waiting on BACK TO MAP. Escape then pauses (and QUIT returns). Not blocking but disorienting: the ending dialogue loop leaks a key press into the Replay.

- Ch12 clear screen has "☆" for the star row in the text dump while Best shows ★☆☆: show "Helping Hand used" next to the stars so you know why.

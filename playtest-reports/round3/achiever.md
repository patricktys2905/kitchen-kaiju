# Playtest evaluation — The Achiever

**Fun score: 7 / 10** (Records is a clear, motivating checklist and grinding is fast; but I couldn't see unlocks happen, and the endgame achievements look like long, unguided grinds)

## What I did
- Title -> Records (0/32), Kaiju Gallery, Free Play (1v1 and 4-player), Party Modes (Team Feast), Daily (Hot Dumpling), Story ch.1 (3 stars), Gauntlet x2 (best wave 3).
- ~25 matches total (about 10 by hand/auto:hard, the rest via /autoplay hard). Ended at 11/32 achievements, 18/18 tables, 16/20 kaiju, 3 story stars, 1-day daily streak. 3 colours unlocked within the first ~8 matches.
- ~75 curl calls; /errors: none.

## What matched my goals / what was fun
- Records page is excellent for me: every achievement shows its requirement plainly, colour rewards are tagged with a palette icon, and "Next up: X (86%)" at the bottom tells me what to chase. Progress counters ("2/3", "8/25") on in-progress ones.
- Early achievements pop fast (First Bite, Crumb Muncher, World Tour came within ~10 matches). Tables visited counter reaching 18/18 naturally is satisfying.
- Gallery shows collection % (16/20 kaiju, 15/18 tables, 4/11 colours) - a perfect completionist hook. Colours work on every kaiju, so one unlock isn't wasted.
- Gauntlet perk picks between waves (Mustard Coat, Hot Feet, Sugar Rush, Full Belly) add a build feel; Daily gives a streak; Story gives 3-star targets (no rounds lost) and spicy/gift tracks.
- Party mode wins and Daily clear correspond to named achievements - good variety of ways to progress.

## What took too long / was tedious
- /autoplay of 3 matches took ~2m20s (about 45s a match at hard); 25-match, 50-match, 1000-crumb, 250-KO achievements will be a long grind with no way to speed up (no fast-forward when I'm out and only bots remain? there is an x3 fast-forward, which is good, but it's only when bots are left).
- Win rate vs hard bots in 4-player is ~45% for the auto player, so streak achievements (3 and 5 in a row) will take many attempts; my best streak after 20 matches was 3.
- Gauntlet first try: lost wave 1 outright - there's no sense of how many lives/rounds a wave has until it ends.

## What was over too quickly / too shallow
- Story ch.1 is trivially easy (3 stars on first try vs "easy" Crabbage) - fine as a tutorial, but 36 stars total implies many chapters that I couldn't gauge.

## Confusing or unclear
- I never saw an "Achievement unlocked!" toast/banner on the match-end screen (Better Together and First Bite unlocked silently as far as the visible texts/screenshots show). An achiever needs the dopamine hit at the moment it happens, and needs to know a colour was unlocked and where to equip it (equip is in the Kaiju gallery "YOUR COLOUR" row; not discoverable from Records).
- Which achievements are earned by the human only vs bots: the footnote says stats count "people on this device (not bots)" - but with /autoplay my seat is bot-played; unclear to a real player, fine for me.
- Team Feast with 1 bot gives a 1v1 "team" match - counts for the achievement, slightly odd.
- "Win with 8 different kaiju" and "Win a Hot Dumpling match" etc. don't show progress counts like others do on page 1 (only some show X/Y).

## Bugs found
- None. /errors returned "none" at the start, middle and end. Escape from a finished autoplay match returned to menu normally.

## Top 3 changes that would raise my score
1. Add a visible "Achievement unlocked + colour unlocked" toast on the results screen (with a one-tap "equip now"), plus a "new" badge on Records.
2. Show progress counters on every unfinished achievement (kaiju-wins x/8, gauntlet wave, streak) and add a "pin an achievement" to display during matches/results.
3. Shorten the long grinds: faster match-end/fast-forward when the human is eliminated, and let the long-tail achievements (250 KOs, 1000 crumbs, 50 wins) also progress in Gauntlet/Story so every mode feeds the checklist.

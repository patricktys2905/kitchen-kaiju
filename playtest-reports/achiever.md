# Playtest evaluation — The Achiever

**Fun score: 7 / 10** (Records/progress UX is excellent and the grind is rewarding; the 1-vs-1 default and ~40-80s hard-bot matches make the long tail slowish, and goals like "Mix It Up" give me no control)

## What I did
- Title -> Records (0/33), Free Play (joined 1 seat, started with 1 bot), Kaiju Gallery, Party Modes (Team Feast), Story map; ~15 matches total (mostly /autoplay hard), ~12 min of game time. 6/33 achievements by the end.
- Matches played 7+ in Records (5 wins), 40 KOs, 206 crumbs, 19 KAIJU, tables 17/18.

## What matched my goals / what was fun
- Records is superb for an achiever: every achievement shows requirement text, reward colour, a progress bar (e.g. 13/25, 66/100) and a "Next up" line. Stats on the left. Page 2 has more.
- Title screen "NEXT UP" ticker (Clear ch4 -> Minturtle; Score 25 KOs -> Grape colour, with %) gives a clear goal every launch.
- Gallery shows 16/20 kaiju, 15/18 tables, 4/12 colours, and clicking a locked item states exactly how to unlock it ("Beat Story chapter 4", "Ocean: Win 10 matches").
- Early achievements fall quickly (First Bite, Forked Up, Crumb Muncher, Size Matters, On a Roll, World Tour in ~7 matches) - good dopamine curve.
- Gobzilla's 1v1 round felt readable: crumbs -> grab -> throw -> KO worked in 2 rounds.

## What took too long / was tedious
- Hard-bot 4-player matches cost ~40-80 s each; 1,000 crumbs / 250 KOs / 50 wins / 25 matches means 40+ matches (~45 min) of pure grind. Bottomless Belly at 206/1000 after 7 matches ~ 35 more matches.
- "Win 5 in a row", "Win 8 different kaiju", "Win 10 Hot Dumplings popping on others" are luck/RNG-gated; I couldn't see how to pick my kaiju in Free Play (seat showed a random one, e.g. Gobzilla Jr., then Norigon) so Mix It Up (3/8) is out of my hands.
- Story chapter 4 gates a kaiju, but I had no way to grind it quickly (autoplay doesn't do story).

## What was over too quickly / too shallow
- Free Play with 1 bot is over in ~6 s of gameplay per round; first 3 rounds felt thin vs 4 players.
- Early achievements come so fast that the first 10 minutes feel front-loaded; then a long flat stretch.

## Confusing or unclear
- Records page 2 is hidden behind a small PAGE button; I only found it by chance. Page 1 shows 18, page 2 shows 15 - no indication there are 33 across pages other than header.
- Free Play defaults to "First to 5" then my next menu said "First to 3" - setting persisted oddly (maybe autoplay changed it).
- "Tables visited 17/18" yet menu says 15 tables + 3 locked; the 18 is explained nowhere I saw.
- Couldn't tell if a toast appeared when an achievement/colour unlocked (harness text didn't show one).

## Bugs found
- /errors: none.
- Harness-visible oddity: in autoplay with 2 Gobzillas in a match, result said "winner YOU" while a different Gobzilla seat led - likely harness labelling, not a game bug. Party Modes setup (Team Feast) did not seem to persist into autoplay (it launched Free Play), probably harness.

## Top 3 changes that would raise my score
1. Let me pick/lock my kaiju per match in Free Play (so Mix It Up and per-kaiju goals are plannable) and show per-kaiju win progress (x/8) in Gallery.
2. Add faster grinding paths: short "Quick Match" (first to 1-2 rounds) or doubled crumb/KO progress in practice, and a visible "what mode gives most progress per minute" hint; long counters (1,000 crumbs) need a mid-way reward.
3. Make Records pagination obvious (tabs: Combat / Modes / Story) and add an unlock toast recap on the match-end screen ("+Ocean colour, 2 achievements").

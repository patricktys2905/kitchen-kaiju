# Playtest evaluation — The Achiever

**Fun score: 6 / 10** (the Records page is excellent, but the grind is slow, many goals sit behind other modes, and some stats feel unreliable)

## What I did
- Title -> Records (0/29), Free Play setup, 1 manual match (lost), several autoplay batches. About 8 matches in total, 7 logged in Records (2 won, 29% win rate).
- Re-checked Records after each batch (4 times, plus both achievement pages and 2 screenshots). /errors: none.
- Progress after the grind: 4/29 achievements (First Bite, Crumb Muncher, World Tour, plus one more), 15 KAIJU transforms, 323 crumbs, 14 KOs, 16/18 tables.

## What matched my goals / what was fun
- Records is exactly what an achiever wants: 29 achievements over 2 pages with plain-language requirements ("Win 10 matches", "Win with 8 different kaiju") and progress bars with counts (2/10, 176/1000, 9/10). I never had to guess what to do.
- Quick early wins: World Tour (12 tables) and Crumb Muncher unlocked within about 3 matches, so the first session feels rewarding.
- Stats page (matches, rounds, KOs, crumbs, KAIJU count, tables 15/18, favourite kaiju) gives a satisfying sense of tracking.
- The end-of-match awards (Most KOs, Crumb Monster, Survivor, Condiment Hoarder) are fun flavour.

## What took too long / was tedious
- Autoplay matches take roughly 90-120 s of real time each. At a real 30% win rate, "Win 10 matches" is about 30 matches (~50 min) and "Win 50 matches" is a multi-hour grind. "Win 250 KOs" and "1,000 crumbs" are long too, and "Finish 25 matches" is the only quick one.
- The "Win 5 in a row" and "Win with 8 different kaiju" goals need many wins in a row or with a spread of kaiju. Autoplay seems to put me on a random kaiju, so I can't deliberately target Mix It Up.
- Several goals are gated behind other modes (Team Feast, Crumb Rush, Hot Dumpling, Daily, Gauntlet, Story Spicy, Workshop, Party Cup). Records does not say where to find them or how.

## What was over too quickly / too shallow
- Nothing is quick in a way that bothers me, but "Size Matters" (go KAIJU 10 times) was done in about 2 matches, so it's trivial next to the other goals.
- No reward or unlock for hitting an achievement was visible (no cosmetics or colour styles, no title), so the goals feel like pure bragging rights for an achiever who wants colour styles.

## Confusing or unclear
- "Favourite kaiju" showed Gobzilla Jr. after I played Salmonster and Gobzilla Jr., and later Mothball. It isn't clear what it counts.
- My manual play lost often even when following the on-screen advice. The bot in my seat during autoplay won only about 1 in 4, so autoplay doesn't represent a competent player; the 29% win rate makes the wins-based achievements feel far away.
- I couldn't tell whether the "Win with 8 different kaiju" counter only counts wins as the human or as autoplay seats.
- Records shows "Tables visited 15/18", but Free Play says only 15 tables are available ("3 more to unlock in Story"), so the other 3 are hidden requirements.

## Bugs found
- No JS errors from /errors.
- Minor: the autoplay results screen at times didn't list "YOU" among the placings (e.g. it listed BLOBLIN, SLOBA, MOTHBALL, PIZZARAPTOR) because the player's seat is renamed to the random kaiju. Not a bug, but it made it hard to tell whether I won.

## Top 3 changes that would raise my score
1. Show the hint for where to earn each mode-gated achievement (a small "Play Party Modes" or "Story" tag), plus a "recommended next" or "closest" achievement on the Records header.
2. Reward achievements with something visible: colour styles, titles or kaiju unlocks, and show an unlock toast at match end.
3. Cut grind: lower the 50 wins / 250 KOs / 1,000 crumbs targets or add tiered mid-way tiers (5/25/100), and let autoplay (or a player) choose which kaiju is used so "Mix It Up" can be targeted.

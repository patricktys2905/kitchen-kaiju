# Playtest evaluation — The Story Fan

**Fun score: 7 / 10** Charming cast, tight chapter pacing, great loading of tips and twists; but story is thin, early chapters are too easy, then a sudden difficulty cliff, and the scripted fights are over in seconds.

## What I did
- Title -> Story. Opening cutscene (only a dark still + one caption line visible in headless, harness limit), Head Chef intro dialogue.
- Grand Feast chapters 1-6 played myself with "attack": Ch1 (3 stars, ~25s), Ch2, Ch3 (lost 2 rounds first, cleared), Ch4 boss Norigon (cleared, unlocked Minturtle), Ch5, Ch6 (unlocked Freezer Shelf table).
- "Seconds open!" appeared on the title screen after Ch6, and a "SECOND HELPINGS" button appeared on the story map. Played Second Helpings 1 (clear), 2 (clear), 3 "Flipping Out" (MINI KAIJU twist, 3 rivals, first to 3): lost 3 times (twice manually, once with auto:hard, then a 4th loss with auto:hard).
- Back to Grand Feast Ch7 (Short Order Showdown): lost 3-0 manually, lost with auto:hard, then cleared on the 3rd try with a mixed attack/power approach.
- Roughly 30+ matches/rounds; read all dialogue lines and every result screen; ~6 screenshots. /errors: none.

## What matched my goals / what was fun
- Every chapter opens with 2-3 short, in-character lines (Crabbage "This roast is MY turf", Salmonster "Bow before the boss of the belt!", Avocadrake "The chef's cleaver loves a slow kaiju. Are you slow?"). Puns land, lines are short enough to read without boredom.
- Chapter-clear screens each have a rival sign-off ("Worst. Birthday. Ever. *pop*") - a nice little reward for every win.
- Clear unlock cadence: Ch4 gave a kaiju, Ch6 gave a table, stars shown on the map, "Chapter N unlocked!" messaging.
- Each chapter has a distinct table with a different hazard and a hint shown before round 1 ("Burners glow before they FLARE", "Red square = spatula incoming"). Teaches mechanics gradually. The MINI KAIJU twist screen looked great and is a fun idea.
- Boss tip ("Norigon starts every round ready to go KAIJU") is a good bit of design.
- Defeat screen is kind and re-states the chapter tip plus RETRY/MAP buttons.

## What took too long / was tedious
- Dialogue is typewritten and needs 1-2 presses per line; the tip line shows up twice (typing then full), so I pressed Space ~8 times before every match. A "skip all" would help on replays.
- Retrying a lost chapter replays the full intro dialogue each time (3 losses = 3 re-reads).
- Match length in first-to-3 chapters (6 rounds possible) feels long compared to 12-second rounds elsewhere.

## What was over too quickly / too shallow
- Ch1 and Ch2 are won in ~12 seconds a round; the first-to-2 on easy gives 3 stars with just "attack". Story at this point is a tutorial that teaches little.
- Story itself is thin: a villain (Head Chef) is announced and then only rival taunts follow; I never felt a plot beat between chapters 1-6. Rivals say a line and vanish.
- Rounds are so short (belly ~18 after a whole round) that the headline feature (crumbs -> STOMP -> KAIJU) basically never happened in story play for me; I won by throwing cutlery. Kaiju power was rarely relevant.
- Opening cutscene in my harness was a dark still with one line; cannot judge the video.

## Confusing or unclear
- Second Helpings opens after Ch6, but its intro dialogue has the Head Chef say "The Golden Spoon is yours... but the back of the fridge wants SECOND HELPINGS" - I had not won the Golden Spoon yet (Ch12 unfinished). Story-order/spoiler mismatch.
- Difficulty labels are misleading: Second Helpings Ch3 shows easy-ish rivals but is a 4-player free-for-all, MINI KAIJU twist, first to 3; I lost 4 times. Chapter 7 jumps from 2-3 rival "easy" fights to 3 "normal" rivals + burners, first to 3.
- It's unclear to a new player what the "STOMP" requirement/belly progress is, since rounds end before belly is full.
- Hard to tell during play who hits me (the "flip" knockout from spatula appeared in the replay but I couldn't read it from the log until a replay).

## Bugs found
- No JS errors (/errors -> none).
- Minor: the typewriter text shown by the harness is clipped mid-sentence on first press, then completes on next press (expected, but means two presses per line).
- Possible content bug: Second Helpings intro line claims Golden Spoon is already won (see above).

## Top 3 changes that would raise my score
1. Smooth the difficulty cliff: Ch7 and Second Helpings Ch3 (4-player MINI KAIJU, first to 3) feel 2 steps harder than Ch1-6; lower first-to or add a 2-rival version, and show hard chapters with a warning.
2. Make the kaiju fantasy happen in story: longer rounds or faster crumbs/belly in early chapters so players actually STOMP, and make the tutorials require it once.
3. Add a quick-skip for dialogue (one press to skip all lines, auto-skip on retry), and give the story more connecting plot beats (Head Chef appearing between chapters, fix the premature Golden Spoon line).

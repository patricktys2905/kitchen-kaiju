# Playtest evaluation — newbie ("The First-Timer")

**Fun score: 7 / 10** (gorgeous, instantly readable and the practice tutorial is great; long matches and a cluttered setup screen hold it back for a first-timer)

## What I did
- Title screen -> HELP (read Basics + Cutlery tabs) -> clicked PRACTICE from the Help screen: 8-step guided tutorial (move, throw, grab, swipe, eat crumbs, stomp, kaiju power). Finished 8/8.
- Quit via Esc -> pause -> QUIT (dumps you back on Help, not title). Then FREE PLAY: clicked slot 1 to join, slots 2 and 3 to add bots, started with the "1 PLAY" key.
- One full Free Play match vs 2 NORMAL bots: 11 rounds, I lost 4-5 (used the harness auto bot after the first round or so). About 15 screenshots, ~60 calls.

## What matched my goals / what was fun
- Title screen is lovely: big friendly mode buttons, cute cast, "Tiny monsters. Giant dinner." tells me the vibe at once.
- Practice mode checklist at the bottom ("THROW your cutlery / hold & let go: SPACE / A / THROW") is exactly what a beginner needs. Ticks fill as you do things. Kaiju stomp moment (giant strawberry dino roaring) is a real "wow".
- Art/readability on tables is excellent; every table looks different (Lazy Susan, Picnic, Griddle, Breakfast Rush). Round callouts ("GOBZILLA skewered TOASTBAT"), instant replay and fast-forward when only bots are left are nice touches.
- End screen with silly awards (Butterfingers, Crumb Monster, Kaiju Lover) is charming and has a Share button.

## What took too long / was tedious
- "First to 5 rounds" with 3 players ended up 11 rounds long. That is a long first match; a beginner would want ~3 rounds as the default.
- Practice has a 3-second waiting pace between rounds/countdowns and replay banners that I could not skip.

## What was over too quickly / too shallow
- Rounds can end in a few seconds if you get skewered early, then you spectate until the round ends (with auto-fast-forward, which is good, but still idle time).

## Confusing or unclear
- Free Play setup is overloaded: six empty slots all saying "click or THROW to join", a wall of keyboard/gamepad legends, and a bottom row of tiny key-pills (2, M, R, U, H, X, Z, O, 1). As a newbie I had to guess that clicking the first slot joins me, other slots add bots, and "1 PLAY" starts. There is no big obvious PLAY button.
- Clicking the "PLAY" pill with the mouse did not reliably start the game (first click at its label did nothing; it worked on a second try at a slightly different spot). Mouse-first players would struggle; I only succeeded via the "1" key.
- Practice: eating crumbs filled belly to 99, not 100, so the "belly full" step stalled until I ate one more crumb. Stomp key (E) is not obvious until the checklist says so.
- Quitting Practice drops you on the Help screen, not title.
- Help text is a lot of small print; the Moves paragraph runs long. Tutorial teaches it better than the text does.
- Never explained what the little power-up candy/shield icons next to names are, nor what the pink/orange squares (placemats) at the table corners do.

## Bugs found
- /errors: none.
- Minor: PLAY pill click target on Free Play setup flaky (see above); Practice belly 99 vs full 100 stall.

## Top 3 changes that would raise my score
1. Simplify Free Play setup: one big "PLAY" button, auto-add 2 bots to the first player on entry, hide key legends behind a "Controls" toggle.
2. Default to shorter matches (first to 3) for new players, and make the stomp/kaiju step in Practice trigger at >=99 belly.
3. Offer Practice/tutorial prominently on first launch (title screen prompt) and return to title after quitting it, plus a one-line explanation of power-up icons and placemat spawn points.

## What testers want next (the structural asks, by how many profiles)
1. **Make progression mean something** (Collector, Achiever, Kid, First-Timer):
   - Mastery levels should unlock cosmetics (poses, trails, frames).
   - A Chef Rank ladder with visible thresholds.
   - Achievements linked to tokens and orders, with pinnable "contracts" and tiers.
   - Cheaper early shop items and a rotating shop.
2. **A real challenge ladder** (Challenger, Fighter):
   - A Gauntlet course map with checkpoints and published grades.
   - Fixed "Trials" with 3 stars each.
   - A ranked bot league with bot personalities.
   - A Dojo / Move Lab to practise each KAIJU power.
3. **Party Cup as an event** (Host):
   - A winner banner for each game.
   - A double-points final.
   - A podium, awards and rematch at the end.
   - A saved Cup Shelf.
   - A Game Night setup flow with per-seat handicaps.
4. **Story as a campaign** (Story Fan, Kid):
   - Boss fights with phases and their own win conditions.
   - Recurring rivals with "rapport".
   - A picture-first Kid Mode with a Sticker Book.
5. **Rule Lab / Workshop 2.0** (Tinkerer): stackable twists, adjustable settings, test-with-bots, table codes.
6. **Onboarding** (First-Timer): a "First Service" checklist for the first hour and a proper end card for Practice.

## Fixed right after round 6
- **Text overlaps:**
  - Perk cards no longer cover the receipt or the victory art (they sit higher), and the receipt hides while you pick.
  - The awards line always fits on one line.
  - The title's extras row no longer runs into the logo.
  - The Story map panel: "Not cleared yet" and the star legend no longer touch other elements.
  - Achievement toasts moved to the top.
  - The Pantry grid fits 8 items.
  - The House Rules panel dims the screen behind it.
- **Gauntlet:**
  - Pressing 1 on a LIFE LOST screen now retries the wave; it used to start a whole new run.
  - The final screen ignores keys for 1.5 s so the summary isn't skipped.
  - "NEW BEST" now compares against the best before the run started.
  - Grade thresholds are shown at the end, and your lives at each countdown.
  - Long perk lists get their own line.
- **Party Cup:** Team Feast gives every teammate their team's place points.
- **Receipts and tokens:**
  - The mercy tip only shows after a loss.
  - Story defeat cards now show the token and mastery receipt, so losses still pay.
  - The base token pay per match went up from 5 to 8.
  - The Daily medal text explains what the next medal needs.
- **Pantry:** two cheap colours, 🍬 Toffee (40) and 💜 Lilac (70).

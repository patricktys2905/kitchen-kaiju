# Playtest evaluation — The Story Fan

**Fun score: 7 / 10** (charming cast, good dialogue tone and a clear ramp; marred by a boss that auto-bots can't beat, 3-lives-feeling long rounds, and thin story payoffs)

## What I did
- Title -> Story (The Grand Feast). Watched the opening cutscene (a still with a big "one table. one..." caption), read pre-match dialogue for chapters 1-6 and the post-match "CHAPTER CLEAR" quips.
- Cleared chapters 1-6 of The Grand Feast: ch1 lost once (3-round loss to easy Crabbage while playing sloppily), then 3 stars; ch2, 3 cleared; ch4 (Boss of the Belt, Norigon) lost 3 times (two auto:hard attempts, one long manual attempt) then won by hand; ch5 cleared first try; ch6 lost once, cleared second.
- Got the "Freezer Shelf" table unlock on ch6, saw the SECOND HELPINGS button appear on the story screen and cleared its chapter 1 (Smoothie Operators). Checked /errors: none.
- Roughly 20 matches, ~100 tool calls.

## What matched my goals / what was fun
- Dialogue is short, funny and in character ("Four cups, two of us, one of you. Do the maths!", "Worst. Birthday. Ever. *pop*", "Guac... and roll"). Every rival has a voice and a one-liner on defeat.
- Chapter map is easy to read: stars, crowns for bosses, rival portraits with difficulty tag, a hint line, spicy toggle, rewards counter. Story % and star counts give a sense of progress.
- The difficulty ramp is sensible: ch1 easy 1v1, then 3-4 player free-for-alls, new table hazards each chapter (conveyor, wind, cleaver lane, burners) each introduced with a TIP that is actually useful.
- Boss chapter feels like a boss: Norigon starts every round already KAIJU, which forces the "run, then punish" pattern. Beating it by fleeing early and fighting after its kaiju timer expired felt earned.
- Second Helpings unlocking after ch6 with an in-world blurb ("the back of the fridge wants a rematch") and new tables is a nice carrot.
- Chapter clear screen gives star rating and a table unlock reward at ch6.

## What took too long / was tedious
- Rounds-to-win ramp is large: ch3 is first to 3 and the boss went to 5-6 rounds. A boss loss costs 3-4 minutes and the "DEFEATED" screen just repeats the tip.
- The pre-match dialogue replays in full every retry; there is no quick skip (I had to tap through 4-6 boxes after each RETRY). The typewriter also makes you tap twice per box.
- Rounds where I was knocked out early leave me watching bots (there is a fast-forward at least).

## What was over too quickly / too shallow
- Story is mostly a gallery of rival quips; there is no through-line between chapters beyond "Golden Spoon" intro, and the opening cutscene is one still with a caption.
- Post-chapter rewards are sparse early (2/7 rewards by ch6); ch1-5 gave nothing tangible except stars.
- Chapter 1 and 2 are barely a tutorial: the tip tells you to hold THROW but nothing covers stomp/KAIJU powers until ch3.

## Confusing or unclear
- Moving from the clear screen: NEXT worked, but the cursor stays on the previous chapter if you press arrows before the overlay closes, so I replayed ch1 by accident.
- Second Helpings entry is a small button in the top-right corner of the story map; the title screen still says "Ch 7/12" with no hint that a second campaign exists.
- First-to-N varies per chapter (2, 3, 3...) and "round 6" of a first-to-3 shows up in the boss fight without explanation of tie rules (apparently win by 2 or sudden death).
- Difficulty feel: auto:hard (representing good play) lost the boss twice 0-3/0-4, while a careful manual flee-then-fight strategy won. A player doesn't learn that from the tip alone.

## Bugs found
- None from /errors ("none").
- Minor: result text "GOBZILLA ★☆ CRABBAGE ★★" overlay stays on screen until a further input after the match is over (harness may contribute, not clearly a game flaw).

## Top 3 changes that would raise my score
1. Make bosses beatable by sensible play and teach the counter: give a "BOSS PHASE" cue (kaiju timer bar on the boss), shorten the boss to first-to-2 or add a mid-fight checkpoint, and let me skip pre-match dialogue on retry.
2. Add more story between fights: short comic cutscenes after each boss and a real animated opening, plus a tangible reward (table/kaiju/colour) every chapter, not only ch6.
3. Surface Second Helpings (and the Spicy mode) on the main menu/title tile after ch6 and show progress ("Second Helpings unlocked!") so the 2nd campaign is not hidden behind a corner button.

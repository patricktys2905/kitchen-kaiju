# Playtest evaluation — challenger (The Challenge Seeker)

**Fun score: 6 / 10** (the story boss and Spicy have real teeth and good hints, but Gauntlet, Daily and the early chapters fall over too easily for a hard-content player.)

## What I did
- Gauntlet: cleared waves 1-8 (boss wave 5 included) and died on wave 9 to 5 rivals. Waves 1-8 were each one round and mostly won within 3-6 seconds.
- Daily (card reads DAILY / HOT DUMPLING): played part of a 4-player Hot Dumpling round on Fondue Night, then left.
- Story: cleared ch1 (3 stars), ch2 (3 stars) and ch3 (2 stars) by hand. Lost the ch4 boss "Boss of the Belt" twice by hand. Toggled Spicy on ch1 and cleared it (2 flames).
- One /autoplay story match (ch2) was used for speed. Its Chapter Clear screen did not save progress at first, and autoplay then fell back to a Free Play match with me as Bloblin (harness quirk, not counted against the game).
- Dessert Course was not reachable (Story is 16% complete, so I assume it is locked behind the story). Free Play hard bots were not played separately; Story/Gauntlet used the same bots.
- About 105 calls. Records after the session: 19 matches, 15 won, Gauntlet best wave 8, /errors: none.

## What matched my goals / what was fun
- The ch4 boss is the best moment. "Norigon starts every round ready to go KAIJU" is a clear, learnable threat, and the defeat card tells you how to counter it ("keep moving, use the conveyor"). Fleeing the opening KAIJU, picking off the small rival, then fighting the boss after his KAIJU timer ends won me a round. Giving 0 wins and then a close fight felt fair.
- Gauntlet wave 5 shows a boss crown, and each wave shows a rotating table tip and a new table hazard (Fondue shadows, Mustard Shield). Variety per wave is good.
- Spicy ("tougher rivals") is a clean opt-in per chapter with a flame counter (🌶️ 1/12), plus a new reward and "Feeling Spicy!" achievement. That is a good reason to come back.
- Records, achievements (9/29) and story stars (★8/36) give long-term goals.

## What took too long / was tedious
- Story chapters need a Space press twice per dialogue box (typewriter, then advance) before every match. Replays of a chapter repeat the whole dialogue.
- Boss match ran to 6+ rounds (first to 3), which drags when losing.

## What was over too quickly / too shallow
- Gauntlet waves 1-8 are single-round and trivial for a skilled player. Wave 1 is a 1v1 against an unarmed rival and ends in about 3 seconds. Wave 8 with 4 rivals still fell to a basic "attack" loop with bots eliminating each other. The real difficulty only shows at wave 9 (5 rivals), so the first 8 waves are a long warm-up with no stakes.
- Ch1-3 are easy; ch2 took two rounds with no damage. Spicy ch1 was still won 2-0 with 2 KOs and one round lost, so "tougher" is a modest bump on early chapters.
- Daily just seems to be the day's party mode (Hot Dumpling here), with no visible target score, leaderboard or one-attempt framing, so I got no "beat today's challenge" feeling.

## Confusing or unclear
- The title card labelled DAILY has "HOT DUMPLING" as its subtitle, and the in-game banner says only "HOT DUMPLING", so I could not tell it was the daily challenge. Nothing explains the daily rules or reward.
- Story tile clicks are inconsistent: clicking tile 1 left chapter 2 selected, so I used arrow keys. After a clear, selection jumps to the next chapter. Chapter 4's boss "Boss of the Belt" is not marked clearly as locked-until-ch3 until you try.
- No indication how to unlock Dessert Course from the title or Story screens.

## Bugs found
- Possible: /autoplay story clears show "CHAPTER CLEAR!" but Story stayed 5% complete (ch2 not saved) until I cleared ch2 by hand. Likely a harness quirk (the result modal needs NEXT/MAP clicked), so treat as unconfirmed.
- /errors: none.
- Screenshots: art-src/.playtest/7108/shot-4.png (boss defeat), shot-6.png (defeat card with boss hint), shot-3.png (Chapter Clear modal).

## Top 3 changes that would raise my score
1. Make Gauntlet ramp from the start (multi-round waves or tougher bots and more hazards early, start at wave 5 for veterans), so the first 8 waves are not a bore.
2. Give Daily a real identity: a clearly labelled daily challenge with a fixed rule set, a target or score to beat, a streak/reward and a one-shot feel.
3. Make Spicy meaningfully harder (e.g. modifiers on top of tougher AI, boss-like behaviour on normal chapters) and show a Dessert Course unlock hint, plus a skip-dialogue option on replays.
